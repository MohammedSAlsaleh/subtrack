## 1. Assumptions & Methodology

### Key Assumptions
| Assumption | Value | Rationale |
|------------|-------|-----------|
| Launch date | Q3 2026 | App Store & Play Store submission complete |
| Addressable market (KSA) | 12,000,000 potential users | Saudi smartphone users aged 18–45 |
| Annual market growth | 18 % | MENA digital subscription CAGR |
| Free-to-Premium conversion | 5 % Month 1 → 8 % by Month 18 | Industry benchmark: 4–10 % for freemium finance apps |
| Annual plan uptake (of Premium) | 35 % | Price sensitivity; monthly dominant early |
| Monthly churn (Premium) | 4 % Month 1 → 2.5 % by Month 18 | Retention improves with product maturity |
| Affiliate revenue per conversion | SAR 10 avg | Conservative; scales with user base |
| B2B licence (from Year 2) | SAR 120,000/partner/year | 2 partners Year 2, 5 partners Year 3 |
| Platform fee (Apple/Google) | 30 % of IAP revenue | Standard App Store cut |
| VAT (ZATCA) | 15 % on net revenue | Digital services in KSA |
