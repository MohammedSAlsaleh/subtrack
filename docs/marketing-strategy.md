## 2. Target Audience

### Primary Segment — "The Overspender" (Ages 22–35)
- Young Saudi professionals and university graduates
- 4–8 active subscriptions (Netflix, Shahid, Spotify, iCloud, STC TV, Adobe…)
- Pays BNPL for electronics / fashion (Tamara, Tabby)
- Pain: "I have no idea how much I spend every month"
- Channels: TikTok, Instagram Reels, Snapchat

### Secondary Segment — "The Organiser" (Ages 30–45)
- Family breadwinner managing household finances
- Tracks electricity, water, internet, phone bills
- Concerned about savings goals (Hajj fund, emergency fund, car down payment)
- Pain: "My bills come at different times and I always miss one"
- Channels: YouTube, X (Twitter), LinkedIn

### Tertiary Segment — "The Side Hustler" (Ages 25–40)
- Freelancer or SMB owner tracking business subscriptions
- Wants expense reports and CSV export
- Pain: "I need to know what's deductible"
- Channels: LinkedIn, Reddit, productivity communities

## 4. Channel Strategy

### 4.1 Organic / Content Marketing

**Arabic YouTube (highest priority)**
- Weekly 5–8 min videos: "كيف تتحكم في اشتراكاتك الشهرية؟"
- Collaboration with Saudi personal-finance channels (200K–2M subscribers)
- Goal: 3 sponsored integrations within 90 days of launch

**TikTok & Instagram Reels**
- Short-form "shock content": "تدفع كذا كل شهر؟" (revelation of hidden spend)
- User-generated content: screenshot of dashboard showing first-time totals
- Target: 2 viral posts per month (>100K views)

**X / Twitter (Arabic)**
- Daily money tips in Arabic
- Thread style: "5 اشتراكات تدفعها مجاناً بحساب طالب"
- Engage personal-finance community (#ماليتي)

**App Store Optimisation (ASO)**
- Primary KW (EN): subscription tracker Saudi, bill tracker KSA, expense manager Arabic
- Primary KW (AR): تتبع الاشتراكات، مصاريف شهرية، ميزانية
- Screenshots in both languages; Arabic screenshots set as primary

### 4.3 Influencer Marketing

**Tier 1 — Macro (1M+ followers):** 1 integration per quarter, SAR 25,000–50,000 each
**Tier 2 — Mid (100K–1M):** 2 per month, SAR 5,000–15,000 each

Preferred categories: Personal finance, productivity, tech reviews.
Key targets: Mstdfr, Hisab (YouTube), Saudi Finance creators on Snapchat.

### 4.4 Referral Programme

- **Mechanism:** Share a unique link. Friend downloads + registers → both get 30 days free Premium.
- **Viral coefficient target:** K = 0.4 (each user brings 0.4 new users on average)
- **In-app prompt:** Shown after user's first "insight moment" (e.g. total spend revealed on Analytics screen)

## 5. Launch Campaign — "كم تدفع فعلاً؟"
*(How much are you actually paying?)*

**Concept:** A 2-week pre-launch countdown where the app reveals how much the average Saudi spends on subscriptions they forgot about.

**Execution:**
1. Week –2: Teaser posts with blur/redacted numbers → "الكشف قريب"
2. Week –1: Influencer unboxing of Premium access → share their own spend total
3. Launch day: Countdown timer on landing page + mass email to waitlist
4. Week +1: User-generated posts with #كم_تدفع + SAR giveaway (3 × 1-year Premium)

**KPI:** 5,000 downloads in first 7 days.

## 6. Retention & Lifecycle Marketing

| Lifecycle Stage | Trigger | Action |
|----------------|---------|--------|
| Onboarding (Day 0–3) | Sign up | Welcome email series (3 emails, Arabic + English) |
| Activation | First subscription added | In-app celebration + prompt to add more |
| Engagement | 7 days inactive | Push: "لديك فاتورة قادمة خلال 3 أيام" |
| Conversion | Day 28 (trial end) | Email: Premium offer + 15 % discount code |
| Churn risk | No login for 14 days | Re-engagement push: new feature highlight |
| Renewal | 7 days before annual renewal | "Year in Review" personalised email |

**Push notification strategy:**
- Renewal reminder: 7 days, 3 days, 1 day before charge
- Monthly spend report: 1st of each month
- Savings goal milestone: when goal reaches 25/50/75/100 %
